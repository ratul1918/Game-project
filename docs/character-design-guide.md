# The LightHouse — Character Design & Specification Guide

**Subject:** Characters, Enemies, and Entity Specifications  
**Tone:** Tragic, supernatural maritime horror, psychological guilt  

---

## 1. Protagonist: Daniel Hale

### Narrative Context
Daniel Hale is a guilt-haunted former merchant sailor in his mid-to-late thirties. Months before arriving at Harrow Rock, Daniel abandoned six sailors during a ferocious gale to save himself. His guilt is visceral; the harbor board secretly selected him as the replacement keeper because his despair makes him the perfect fodder for Harrow Rock's supernatural leash.

### Visual Appearance
- **Physique:** Lean, weathered, rugged sailor build; slight stoop from exhaustion.
- **Face:** Haggard eyes with dark circles, wind-burned complexion, salt-crusted 3-day stubble, dark unkempt hair plastered by rain.
- **Attire:**
  - Heavy dark waterproof oilskin jacket with brass toggle clasps and wool collar lining.
  - Salt-stained coarse wool sweater underneath.
  - Dark canvas work trousers tucked into thick, greased leather sea boots.
- **First-Person View:**
  - Wet oilskin sleeves visible at camera edges.
  - Calloused, stained hands gripping tools or weapons.
  - Contextual hand animations: reaching for doorknobs, wiping rain from eyes, clutching bleeding wounds.

---

## 2. Antagonist & Father: Thomas Crane (The Keeper's Shadow)

### Narrative Context
Thomas Crane was Harrow Rock's keeper for 23 years. When the sea demanded his estranged son Daniel to pay for his past sins, Crane walked into the ocean to offer his own soul in Daniel's place. The sea swallowed him but cursed him: he stalks the island as a drowned sentinel, desperately trying to herd his son toward the boat shed and away from the hungry waters.

### Human Crane (Flashbacks & Photographs)
- Stately, grey-bearded maritime keeper in formal lighthouse service uniform.
- Tired, affectionate eyes holding a photograph of a young Daniel.

### Drowned Crane (The Stalker)
- **Silhouette:** Imposing, waterlogged figure draped in a rotting keeper's greatcoat that drips seawater continuously.
- **Features:** Pallid, soaked flesh with patches of brine encrustation; face partially obscured by a soaked wool captain's cap and dripping hair.
- **Behavior & Audio:**
  - Moves with deliberate, heavy, aquatic footsteps.
  - Avoids direct confrontation unless provoked by noise.
  - Constantly hums the fragmented maritime lullaby he used to sing to young Daniel.
  - Slams doors and throws crates during the Act 5 finale to divert the Drowned horde away from his fleeing son.

---

## 3. The Radio Voice: Marta

### Character Profile
Marta presents herself over the radio as the harbor relay operator. She speaks with calm, efficient authority, greeting Daniel by name before he introduces himself. In Act 4, Daniel discovers that Marta is not alive; she is an old magnetic reel-to-reel audio loop prepared by the harbor board to guide guilty keepers into position.

### Audio Design
- *Early Acts:* Crisp, authoritative maritime VHF transmission with slight atmospheric static.
- *Later Acts:* Noticeable analog tape wow, flutter, periodic loop clicks, and hollow prerecorded cadence.

## 4. The Drowned: Enemies of Harrow Rock

The Drowned are the waterlogged passengers and sailors of the *Marguerite*, a vessel deliberately lured onto the jagged reefs by island wreckers decades earlier. Trapped between life and the abyss, they are held at bay only by the blazing light of the Fresnel beacon.

### 4.1 The Drifter (Common Drowned)
- **Appearance:** Pale, sodden corpses in shredded 19th-century sailor garments; seaweed tangled in limbs; translucent, bloated skin.
- **Behavior:** Slow wandering patrol; investigates suspicious noises; staggers upon receiving heavy axe impacts.

### 4.2 The Listener (Sound-Sensitive Stalker)
- **Appearance:** Eyes encrusted shut with calcified sea salt and barnacles; elongated fingers with torn nails.
- **Behavior:** Completely blind to line of sight. Possesses hypersensitive hearing, reacting instantly to running footsteps, broken glass, or weapon strikes. Must be bypassed by crouching and slow walking.

### 4.3 The Grasped (Heavy Drowned)
- **Appearance:** Massive drowned stoker or boatswain swollen with sea water, wearing water-heavy leather aprons.
- **Behavior:** High health pool, resistant to standard stagger. Executes a crushing grapple that requires rapid button breakout; capable of breaking through wooden barricades.

### 4.4 The Shallows (Weeping Statues of the Shore)
- **Appearance:** Gaunt, pale figures standing ankle-deep in the surf, staring silently at the tower.
- **Behavior:** Follow the "Quantum Weeping Angel" rule: completely frozen in place whenever Daniel has direct line of sight; advance inexorably across the rocks and courtyard when the player turns away or the lamp flickers out.
