# Harrow Rock Roadmap

The project advances only through playable vertical slices. Each stage ends in a build that can be played from its beginning through its newest content. Scope is cut before a stage is allowed to break the build.

## Step 3 — Thin slice

**Proof:** Press Play, walk through a greybox lighthouse tower, crank the lamp, and cause a placeholder cube stalker to investigate the crank noise.

This slice proves the new Input System, interaction boundary, Lamp state, `NoiseEmitted` contract, noise routing, and a minimal StalkerAI hear/investigate loop. Boxes and debug presentation are sufficient.

## Act 1 — Arrival

Build the ferry-to-first-light sequence in `chapter1-script.md`: boatman warning, tower entry, hot meal, Marta's greeting, and first lamp lighting.

## Act 2 — Cellar and Stalker

Add low fuel, cellar descent, shanty, impossible footprints, the sound-hunting stalker, and the first unobserved Drowned advance.

## Act 3 — Storm and Crank

Kill the generator, make hand-cranking the loud source of lamp power, and create the core pressure between light safety and sound danger.

## Act 4 — Truth

Deliver the *Marguerite*, wreckers, lighthouse leash, Marta recording, ledger, family photograph, and Crane's bargain as an explorable chain of revelations.

## Act 5 — Dawn

Deliver the flooded-courtyard finale, Crane's protection and lullaby, first-light escape, morning boat, and next-keeper epilogue.

## Polish

Only after the complete game is playable: replace proven placeholders, tune pacing and readability, improve audio and visuals, address accessibility, optimize, fix defects, and prepare the release build. Polish does not add acts or major mechanics.
