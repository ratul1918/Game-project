# Lighthouse level layout

The shared lighthouse greybox is generated by `LighthouseLayoutBuilder`. Measurements are approximate Unity metres and are serialized in `LighthouseLayoutSettings.asset`. The builder is re-runnable and preserves gameplay-facing names by rebuilding the complete scene through editor APIs.

## Traversal and gameplay placement

- The hollow tower uses per-segment ring colliders, a 1.1 m spiral stair with 0.18 m risers, landings, handrails, and an outside iron stair from courtyard to gallery.
- The keeper house has real wall gaps at the front double door and tower connection. Ground-floor rooms form a loop through entry hall, keeper room, kitchen, storeroom, and generator passage. The courtyard and boat shed provide an exterior bypass.
- Hiding spots are placed in the keeper room, pantry, storeroom, cellar, flooded corridor, bedroom/library, Level 2 landing, Level 3 landing, Crane's room, lantern room, and boat shed.
- Windows on every occupied floor face the courtyard and shallows. Window geometry uses the `Window` tag and keeps sightlines open for Drowned observation; the ring has no solid interior fill.
- Per-floor patrol points are rebuilt under `Layout/Waypoints`. Stair transitions receive bidirectional `NavMeshLink` components; the final NavMesh remains an explicit per-act bake step.
- `RoomTrigger` volumes publish stable room IDs through `GameEvents.RoomEntered`. They provide room-name/floor-indicator data only and do not reveal a minimap.
- Occlusion areas are generated per floor. Door and window openings receive portals where supported by the installed Unity version.

## Stable gameplay objects

The shared layout does not rename or replace the gameplay identity of `Door`, `Radio`, `MealTable`, `LanternInteract`, `CrankStation`, `HidingSpot`, `CellarHum`, `WetFootprints`, `hidden shelf`, `Ledger`, `Crane's room door`, `FamilyPhoto`, `DoorwayBlock`, `ShorelineEnding`, `boat`, or `figures`. Act builders remain responsible for attaching act-specific scripts and serialized references to these named anchors.

## Surface and rendering contract

Modular wall, floor, door-frame, window, stair, and curved-wall prefabs live in `Assets/_Project/Prefabs/Level`. Shared URP materials are limited to Wood, Stone, Metal, Sand, Water, and a small particle set. Walkable geometry retains the existing Wood, Stone, Metal, Sand, and Water surface tags. Static layout geometry is marked batching-, occlusion-, and navigation-static where applicable.

The F4 performance HUD reports draw calls/batches when Unity runtime statistics are available. Target validation remains 30–60 FPS on the 8 GB M3 MacBook Air.

## Preview exports

Generated greybox previews are stored in [docs/level-previews](./level-previews): courtyard exterior, dock exterior, front-door interior, and top-down floor views for each tower level. They are layout references only, not final art.
