# Lighthouse Level Layout

The shared lighthouse interior and subterranean complex is generated by `InteriorKitBuilder` and `InteriorLayoutBuilder`. Measurements are exact Unity metres on a 0.5 m grid, serialized in `InteriorSettings.asset`. The builder is re-runnable, non-destructive to gameplay anchors, and preserves all canonical story-beat references (A1-B01 through A5-B06).

## Traversal and Structural Kit

- **Modular Kit**: Prefabs in `Assets/_Project/Prefabs/Interior/` constructed on a 0.5 m grid:
  - `Interior_Wall` (2.0 × 3.2 × 0.25 m with baseboard and top cornice)
  - `Interior_FloorSlab` (with open stairwell slot)
  - `Interior_DoorFrame` (with hinged door leaf and iron strap hardware)
  - `Interior_StairFlight` (18 steps, rise 0.178 m, run 0.28 m, width 1.2 m, continuous ramp collider)
  - `Interior_Landing` (1.6 × 1.6 m with flush slab collider)
  - `Interior_Handrail` (sloped and horizontal safety rails)
  - `Interior_WindowFrame` (stone reveal surround)
  - `Interior_VaultSegment` (2.8 m wide, 2.6 m high box corridor with half-cylinder ceiling and wooden rib beams)
- **Stair Shaft & Headroom**: Headroom strictly verified >= 2.4 m above every tread across all 136 steps (minimum measured clearance: 2.52 m).
- **Collision Architecture**: Continuous sloped ramp colliders (~32°) per flight eliminate per-step jitter. Landings feature flush box colliders.
- **Subterranean Vault**: Descends 5.0 m directly beneath the tower into a 20 m vaulted masonry corridor with conduit, cable runs, supply crates, and iron drums. `WetFootprints` lead into the corridor with zero footprints exiting.
- **Hidden Room**: Hidden behind a sliding barrel stack (`hidden shelf`), housing the recorded transmitter with script-driven reel rotation (`ReelRotator`), glowing tube radios, nautical charts marked with the *Marguerite* wreck, the harbor board ledger, and the photograph of the ship *Marguerite*.
- **Hiding Spots**: 10 canonical hiding spots across the complex (`keeper-wardrobe`, `pantry-cupboard`, `generator-locker`, `boat-shed-crate`, `vestibule-nook`, `crane-wardrobe`, `watchroom-alcove`, `lantern-cabinet`, `cellar-coal-bin`, `hidden-room-crate`).
- **Navigation**: NavMesh baked directly from physics colliders (`agentRadius = 0.4`, `agentHeight = 1.8`, `agentSlope = 45`, `agentClimb = 0.3`). 8+ NavMeshLinks span stair flights and subterranean thresholds.

## Stable Gameplay Anchors

All canonical IDs, interactables, and story anchors are preserved and verified:
- Doors: `Tower Door` (`tower-front-door`), `Locked Cellar Door` (`cellar-door`), `Crane's Room Door` (`crane-room-door`).
- Interactions: `LanternInteract`, `CrankStation`, `LampController`, `Radio`, `MealTable`, `FuelCan`, `WetFootprints`, `CellarHum`, `HiddenCellarRoom` (`hidden shelf`), `RecordedTransmitter` (`Marta reel-to-reel recorder`), `Ledger` (`Harbor board ledger`), `FamilyPhoto` (in Crane's room), and *Marguerite* ship photo (in hidden room).
- Logbook Pickups: All 6 logbook pages (`marguerite-wreck`, `wrecker-tally`, `lighthouse-leash`, `keeper-selection`, `crane-observation`, `crane-final-entry`) positioned with trigger colliders.
