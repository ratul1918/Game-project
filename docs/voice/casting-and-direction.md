# Voice Casting & Audio Direction Specification

## Target Soundscape
Set off the North Atlantic coast, 1947. Harrow Rock is a windswept granite spire isolated by rising tides.
Voice acting must balance intimate first-person vulnerability with period-accurate restraint.

---

## Character Casting Profiles

### 1. Daniel Hale (Player Protagonist)
- **Profile**: Male, mid-30s. Former merchant seaman haunted by the sinking of the *Marguerite*.
- **Vocal Quality**: Weary, guarded, intimate, quiet maritime inflection. Low-pitched, strained under breath.
- **Microphone Technique**: Ultra close-mic (Large diaphragm condenser, 3–4 inches from mouth with pop filter). Minimal room reverb, high dry-ratio.
- **Stamina / Foley**: Heavy breathing cycles, breath holds, sharp startle gasps, and physical strain while cranking.

### 2. The Boatman (Charon Archetype)
- **Profile**: Male, late 60s. Weather-beaten ferryman who has ferried many keepers to Harrow Rock.
- **Vocal Quality**: Gruff, gravelly, dry coastal stoicism. Speaks with the weight of someone who knows what happens after dark.
- **Delivery**: Measured, blunt, zero sentimentality. Parting words are factual, not emotional.

### 3. Marta (Harbor Relay Operator / Secret Tape)
- **Profile**: Female, 40s. Harbor board civil servant whose voice guides Daniel via radio.
- **Vocal Quality**: Clinical, crisp, flat, unnervingly calm. Transmit tone has a subtle bureaucratic detachment.
- **Acoustic Treatment**: Radio group bandpass filter (400 Hz HPF, 3500 Hz LPF), faint 60 Hz hum, mild analog tube saturation.
- **Identity Constraint**: Act 1 live radio transmission and Act 4 hidden cellar reel-to-reel tape are the identical recording.

### 4. Thomas Crane (The Stalker / Daniel's Father)
- **Profile**: Male, late 50s. The former keeper consumed by the sea, now a drowned thing in a heavy keeper's oilskin.
- **Vocal Quality**: Non-verbal respirations, raspy wet trachea breathing, baritone humming of 'Leave Her, Johnny'.
- **Emotional Arc**: Begins as an acoustic terror stalker in the dark cellar; resolves into a grieving father sacrificing himself at dawn.

### 5. The Drowned (Chorus of the Sea)
- **Profile**: Spectral chorus of drowned sailors lurking in the shallows.
- **Vocal Quality**: Cold, layered whispering in multiple voices; siren-like lure harmonies carrying across the breaking waves.
- **Acoustic Treatment**: Long cavernous reverb tail, high wet-mix, slight stereo phase modulation.
